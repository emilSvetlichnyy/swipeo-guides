Swipe photo cleaner apps for iPhone compared

Many apps use the same swipe idea. These are the differences worth checking.

Checked 27 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerSwipe cleaners show one photo at a time: swipe to keep or delete. Examples are Swipewipe (month-by-month review), Slidebox (album sorting), Pixtide Swipe (one-time Pro purchase) and Swipeo (iPhone and iPad, undo, in-app trash, duplicate groups, storage statistics). They differ in trash or undo, sorting (by month or category), free limits and privacy. Facts below come from App Store listings (checked 21 Nov 2026; Swipeo row 27 Nov 2026) and may change.

How to choose

  • Pick an app with a trash or undo.
  • Read the privacy label and check what happens with photo access.
  • Check the free limit so you are not surprised mid-session.
  • Look at recent reviews and update dates: many listings are new with similar names.

Doing this with Swipeo

You are reading a page by the developer of Swipeo. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Which swipe cleaner is the best?

There is no neutral winner. Compare trash, privacy, limits and price against your needs.

Do swipe apps delete permanently?

Safer ones hold items in a trash first. After that, iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Is Swipeo free?

It has a free tier with a daily limit on deletions; Premium removes the limit.

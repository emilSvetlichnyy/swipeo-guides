Swipe photo cleaner apps for iPhone compared

Many apps use the same swipe idea. These are the differences worth checking.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerSwipe cleaners show one photo at a time: swipe to keep or delete. They differ in trash or undo, sorting (by month or category), free limits and privacy. Facts below come from each App Store listing as of 21 Nov 2026 and may change.
App (listing)What the listing says

Photo Delete Swipe: SwiperSwiped-away photos go to a trash area before final deletion
Photo Cleaner: SwipeAwayLeft to delete, right to keep; month-by-month timeline
SwipeoSwipe or tap to keep or delete; in-app trash; duplicate groups (not AI); review sets such as screenshots, bursts, Live Photos and large videos; free daily limit, Premium removes it

How to choose

  • Pick an app with a trash or undo.
  • Read the privacy label and check what happens with photo access.
  • Check the free limit so you are not surprised mid-session.
  • Look at recent reviews and update dates: many listings are new with similar names.

FAQ

Which swipe cleaner is the best?

There is no neutral winner. Compare trash, privacy, limits and price against your needs.

Do swipe apps delete permanently?

Safer ones hold items in a trash first. After that, iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Is Swipeo free?

It has a free tier with a daily limit on deletions; Premium removes the limit.

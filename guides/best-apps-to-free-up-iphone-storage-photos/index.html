iPhone storage full of photos: which cleaner app should you use?

Pick by how you want to decide: let an app group the clutter, or review photos yourself one by one.

Checked 27 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerTry the free steps first: empty Recently Deleted, merge Duplicates and delete screenshots and large videos. If that is not enough, there are two kinds of apps: all-in-one cleaners that scan and group (Clever Cleaner, CleanMy®Phone) and swipe cleaners where you keep or delete one photo at a time (Slidebox, Pixtide Swipe, Odoa, Swipeo). How to choose

  • You want it done in a few taps: an all-in-one cleaner groups the clutter for you; still review what it selects.
  • You want control: a swipe cleaner shows each photo before it goes, ideally with an undo or trash.
  • You worry about privacy: read the App Privacy label and look for on-device processing.
  • Space not coming back: deleted photos still use space until Recently Deleted is emptied. See storage not freeing after deleting photos.

FAQ

Do I need an app to free up iPhone storage?

Not always. Emptying Recently Deleted, merging duplicates and removing large videos and screenshots can free a lot with built-in tools.

What is the difference between all-in-one and swipe cleaners?

All-in-one cleaners scan and group clutter for you. Swipe cleaners show one photo at a time and you choose keep or delete.

Why is my storage still full after deleting photos?

Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use space until you delete them there.

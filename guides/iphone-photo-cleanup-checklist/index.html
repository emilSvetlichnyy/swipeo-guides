iPhone photo cleanup checklist: what to delete first

Highest clutter and lowest regret first, so the first 15 minutes already help.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerDelete in this order: screenshots, screen recordings, duplicates, bursts, large videos, then empty Recently Deleted. Check iCloud Photos before you start, because deleting there deletes everywhere.

Steps

  1. Check Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos (on means deletes sync everywhere).
  2. Albums → Media Types → Screenshots: delete.
  3. Media Types → Screen Recordings: delete.
  4. Utilities → Duplicates: Merge.
  5. Media Types → Bursts: keep only favorites.
  6. Media Types → Videos: delete long ones you do not need.
  7. Utilities → Recently Deleted: Delete All.

FAQ

Which step frees the most space?

Usually large videos, then duplicates and bursts. It depends on your library.

How often should I do this?

Every few months, or monthly for heavy camera users.

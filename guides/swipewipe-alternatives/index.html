Swipewipe alternatives for iPhone

Pick by what you miss: iPad support, albums, a one-time purchase or a swipe review with a trash.

Checked 27 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerFour options: Swipeo for iPhone and iPad with undo, an in-app trash and storage statistics; Slidebox if you also sort into albums; Pixtide Swipe if you want a one-time purchase instead of a subscription; Odoa for month-by-month progress with albums.

Doing this with Swipeo

Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Which Swipewipe alternative works on iPad?

Swipeo lists iPhone and iPad. Pixtide Swipe and Odoa list iPhone but not iPad. Check the Slidebox listing for its devices.

Is there an alternative without a subscription?

Pixtide Swipe lists a one-time Pro purchase and no subscription. Slidebox lists a one-time purchase option.

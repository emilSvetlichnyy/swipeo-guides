How to declutter a huge iPhone photo library

Clean first, organize second, and work in small time-boxed sessions.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerStart with the obvious junk (screenshots, duplicates, bursts, accidental shots), then go month by month in short sessions, mark keepers with a heart, and only then organize albums. Reviewing around 30 photos a minute means 1,000 photos take about half an hour.

Steps

  1. Delete screenshots and screen recordings you no longer need.
  2. Merge exact duplicates and trim bursts.
  3. Go through months one at a time. Stop after a set time.
  4. Heart your best photos so they are easy to find.
  5. Create a few albums for what remains (family, trips).
  6. Empty Recently Deleted.

Cleaning first matters: organizing a library full of junk only hides the junk. Face and place grouping in Photos then does most of the organizing for you.

Doing this with Swipeo

FAQ

How long does it take to clean 20,000 photos?

At roughly 30 photos a minute, about 11 hours total. Splitting it into 20-minute sessions makes it doable.

What should I delete first?

Screenshots, duplicates and bursts: highest clutter, lowest regret.

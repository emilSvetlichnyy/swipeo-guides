Swipeo vs Slidebox

Two swipe photo cleaners with different centers: Slidebox is also an album organizer, Swipeo is built around keep, delete and a trash.

Checked 23 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerBoth let you swipe to keep or delete photos and have undo. Slidebox adds an album organizer and says changes sync with iCloud through the Photos app. Swipeo adds an in-app trash with restore, duplicate groups and storage statistics, and says it does not upload to the cloud. Which fits you

  • You also want albums: Slidebox is built for sorting photos into albums as you clean.
  • You want a recoverable step before iOS: Swipeo's trash lets you restore a photo you swiped by mistake.
  • You care about duplicates: both offer a way to compare; the approach differs.

We make Swipeo, so weigh our column accordingly. The Slidebox column only repeats its listing; we did not test it.

Doing this with Swipeo

Swipeo reviews your own Photos library one card at a time and keeps deleted items in its trash first. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone. The free version has a daily limit on deletions. Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Is Slidebox or Swipeo better?

There is no neutral winner. Slidebox suits album organizing; Swipeo suits keep-or-delete review with a trash and duplicate groups.

Do both apps delete photos permanently?

Deleted photos go through iOS Recently Deleted, which keeps them for 30 days. Swipeo adds its own trash before that step.

Are both free?

Both can be started free. Check each listing for what is included in the free tier and in the paid options.

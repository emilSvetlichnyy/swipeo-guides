Photo cleaner apps that don't upload your photos

Look for two things: a statement that photos stay on the device, and an App Privacy label you are comfortable with.

Checked 27 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerSwipeo, Odoa, Pixtide Swipe and Clever Cleaner each say in their App Store listing that photos are processed on the device or not uploaded. Their App Privacy labels differ: not uploading photos does not mean collecting no data.
AppWhat the listing says about photosApp Privacy label (summary)
SwipeoCleanup runs on device; no cloud uploadData not linked to you: identifiers, usage data, diagnostics
OdoaWorks on your iPhone; photos are never uploaded to their serversData not linked to you: purchases, usage data, identifiers, diagnostics
Pixtide SwipeYour photo library never leaves your phoneIncludes data linked to you, among it purchases, identifiers and usage data
Clever CleanerEverything is processed locally; photos and videos stay on your deviceIncludes identifiers used to track you

How to check yourself

  • Read App Privacy on the listing.
  • Give Limited Photos access first if you are unsure.
  • Remember that iCloud Photos is Apple's own upload: it works regardless of the cleaner you use.

These are developer statements and labels as of 27 Nov 2026, not an independent audit. We make Swipeo and did not test the other apps.

Doing this with Swipeo

Swipeo cleans the library on your device and holds deleted items in its own trash first.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Does Swipeo upload photos to a server?

Swipeo does not upload your photos to its own servers. It uses analytics and subscription services.

Is the App Privacy label verified by Apple?

Apple states that the label is provided by the developer. Treat it as the developer's declaration.

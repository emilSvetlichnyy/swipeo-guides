Best apps to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

Start with the free built-in tool. Add an app only if you need similar photos, not just exact copies.

Checked 23 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answeriPhone already has Photos → Albums → Utilities → Duplicates → Merge, which handles exact or near-exact copies. Apps add similar-photo groups (a burst of nearly identical shots) and a pick-the-best step. Compare how each app reviews before deleting, whether it can undo or use a trash, and the price model. How to choose

  • Exact copies only: the built-in Duplicates album is enough and costs nothing.
  • Many near-identical shots: an app with similar-photo groups saves time, because Apple's tool does not count shots taken seconds apart.
  • You want to decide yourself: prefer one-by-one review and a trash or undo step over one-tap delete everything.
  • Check the price model on the listing before you start: free tier, subscription or one-time purchase.

FAQ

Does iPhone have a built-in duplicate finder?

Yes. Open Photos, then Albums, then Utilities, then Duplicates, and tap Merge. It handles exact or near-exact copies.

Which app finds similar photos, not just exact duplicates?

Clever Cleaner and CleanMy®Phone list similar-photo detection; Remo lists images that look almost the same; Slidebox lets you compare similar photos by swiping. Check each listing for the current features.

Is there a neutral best app?

No. It depends on whether you want automatic grouping or one-by-one review, and on the price model you accept.

Where do deleted duplicates go?

iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Some apps add their own trash before that step.

How to clean your iPhone photos month by month

One month at a time turns an impossible job into a routine.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerPick one month, open it in Photos' Months view, delete the junk, heart the keepers, then stop. Repeat next session. A time-box (for example 15 minutes) keeps it light and consistent.

Steps

  1. Photos → Library → Months.
  2. Open one month and tap Select.
  3. Delete blurry, duplicate and accidental shots; heart the best.
  4. Note the month you finished so you can continue next time.

Working by period keeps context fresh: you remember what happened that month, so decisions are quicker and less emotional.

Doing this with Swipeo

Swipeo has a Continue where you left list and an On this day set, so you can pick up a cleaning session where it stopped.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Should I start with the oldest or newest month?

Newest is easier because you remember it. Oldest often has the biggest clutter. Either works if you stay consistent.

Is there an automatic way?

Apps with a Continue where you left feature help. Photos itself does not track your progress.

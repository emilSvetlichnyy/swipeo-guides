How to delete multiple photos on iPhone at once

Select many photos in one gesture, then empty Recently Deleted to get the space back.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOpen Photos, tap Select, tap photos or drag your finger across several, then tap the Trash button and confirm. Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so empty that album if you want the storage back right away.

Steps

  1. Open the Photos app and go to Library or an album.
  2. Tap Select in the top right.
  3. Tap photos one by one, or slide your finger across a row of thumbnails to select many. Swipe sideways first: swiping vertically scrolls the grid.
  4. Tap the Trash button, then confirm Delete.
  5. To free space now: Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

If selecting in a grid feels risky, go through photos one at a time instead: Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

FAQ

Is there a limit to how many photos I can select?

Apple does not state a hard limit on its Delete photos page. Very large selections can be slow, so work in batches if the phone lags.

Do deleted photos free storage immediately?

No. They stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days unless you delete them there too.

Can I undo a bulk delete?

Yes, within the 30 days: open Recently Deleted, select the items and tap Recover.

How to find large videos on iPhone

Photos has no size sort. These workarounds find the heavy clips.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerPhotos cannot sort by file size. Check the Videos and Screen Recordings albums for long durations, look at Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Photos, or use a cleaner that lists videos by size.

Steps

  1. Open Photos → Albums → Videos and read the duration badges, longest first by eye.
  2. Do the same in Screen Recordings and Slo-mo.
  3. Settings → General → iPhone Storage: check the Photos entry and any storage recommendations.
  4. Optional: use a size-sorting app, and check what access it requests.

Sources disagree on whether any built-in sort exists; most say no, and an old "Review Personal Videos" suggestion is reported as no longer available.

FAQ

Can I sort Photos by size in the Files app?

The Files app sorts files by size, but Photos library items are not normal files there.

Which videos are usually the biggest?

Long 4K clips, slow-motion videos and screen recordings.

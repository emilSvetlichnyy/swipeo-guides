Delete & clean up

Step-by-step ways to delete and find unwanted photos, videos and screenshots on iPhone.

  • How to delete multiple photos on iPhone at onceSelect many photos in one gesture, then empty Recently Deleted to get the space back.
  • How to select all photos on iPhoneThere is no one-tap Select All for the entire library, but albums and drag-select get close.
  • How to delete all photos from iPhoneBack up first, check iCloud Photos, then delete and empty Recently Deleted.
  • How to delete all screenshots on iPhone at onceScreenshots have their own album, so bulk deletion takes under a minute.
  • Delete old screenshots on iPhone with a ShortcutA Shortcut can remove screenshots by age instead of deleting all of them.
  • How to delete duplicate photos on iPhoneThe Duplicates album handles exact copies; similar shots need a manual pass or an app.
  • What does "Merge Duplicates" do on iPhone?It keeps one copy and sends the others to Recently Deleted.
  • How to find and delete similar photos on iPhoneApple's Duplicates album only catches exact copies, so similar shots take a different approach.
  • How to find and delete blurry photos on iPhoneThere is no built-in blur finder, so it is a scroll, a swipe app or a third-party scan.
  • How to delete Live Photos but keep the still photoMake a still copy first, then delete the Live original. Duplicating alone frees nothing.
  • How to delete burst photos on iPhonePick the keepers inside a burst, then remove the rest.
  • How to delete large videos on iPhoneLong videos take the most room. Find them, export what you want to keep, delete the rest.
  • How to find large videos on iPhonePhotos has no size sort. These workarounds find the heavy clips.
  • How to delete screen recordings on iPhoneThey live in their own album, which makes them easy to clear in bulk.
  • How to delete hidden photos on iPhoneThe Hidden album is locked by default: unlock it, delete, and clear Recently Deleted.
  • How to delete large attachments in Messages on iPhoneRemove the heavy media and keep the conversations.
  • How to swipe to delete photos on iPhoneThe Photos app has no swipe-to-delete. Swipe cleaners add it, and a good one includes a trash.

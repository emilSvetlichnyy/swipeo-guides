How to delete hidden photos on iPhone

The Hidden album is locked by default: unlock it, delete, and clear Recently Deleted.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOpen Albums → Utilities → Hidden, unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, tap Select, choose the photos and tap Trash. They then go to Recently Deleted for 30 days unless you delete them there as well.

Steps

  1. Photos → Albums → Utilities → Hidden.
  2. Authenticate (Face ID, Touch ID or passcode).
  3. Tap Select, pick the photos and tap Trash.
  4. Open Recently Deleted (also locked) and delete them for good if you want the space now.

If you only want to hide the album itself, turn off Show Hidden Album in Photos settings. That does not delete any photo. With iCloud Photos on, hiding and deleting sync to your other devices.

Doing this with Swipeo

Swipeo works with the photos in your main library. Hidden items are handled by iOS settings, so unhide them first if you want to review them.

FAQ

Why does Photos ask for Face ID?

Apple locks the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums by default.

Does hiding free storage?

No. Hidden photos still use space until you delete them.

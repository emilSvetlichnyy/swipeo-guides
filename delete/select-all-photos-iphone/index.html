How to select all photos on iPhone

There is no one-tap Select All for the entire library, but albums and drag-select get close.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerApple's Photos app does not describe a whole-library Select All. In albums, tap Select and a Select All option appears at the top left (guides report it is missing in Recents and Imports). In the main grid, tap Select and drag your finger across thumbnails.

Steps

  1. Open Photos → Albums and choose an album such as Screenshots or Videos.
  2. Tap Select, then Select All (top left).
  3. In the main Library grid: tap Select and drag a finger down across thumbnails to extend the selection.
  4. Tap Trash to delete, or Share to move them.

Where it works

PlaceSelect All?
Albums (Screenshots, Videos, Live Photos…)Yes, via Select → Select All
Library → Days viewPer day or week
Recents / whole libraryNo single button; drag-select instead

Source guides disagree slightly on which views offer it, and it can change with iOS updates. If you do not see the button, check on your own iOS version.

FAQ

Why is there no Select All in my Library?

Apple's support pages do not describe one for the whole library. Albums and drag-select are the practical options.

Is selecting everything safe?

Only if you have a backup. Deleting with iCloud Photos on also removes photos from your other devices.

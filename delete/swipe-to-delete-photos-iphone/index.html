How to swipe to delete photos on iPhone

The Photos app has no swipe-to-delete. Swipe cleaners add it, and a good one includes a trash.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerApple's Photos app deletes with Select → Trash; it has no swipe-to-delete. Swipe cleaner apps show one photo at a time: swipe one way to keep, the other to delete. Pick one with a trash or undo step before final deletion.

Steps

  1. Install a swipe cleaner and allow access to your Photos library.
  2. Swipe or tap to keep or delete each photo or video.
  3. Review the app's trash (a safe one holds deleted items before the final step).
  4. Confirm the final deletion in the iOS prompt, then empty Recently Deleted.

FAQ

Is swiping faster than Select?

For deciding photo by photo, yes. For clearing a whole album, Select All is faster.

Can I undo a swipe?

In a swipe app with a trash, yes. After final deletion, iOS keeps items in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

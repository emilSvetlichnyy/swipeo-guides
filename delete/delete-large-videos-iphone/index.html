How to delete large videos on iPhone

Long videos take the most room. Find them, export what you want to keep, delete the rest.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOpen Photos → Albums → Videos and look for the longest clips: duration is the best clue to size, because Photos has no built-in sort by file size. Delete them, then empty Recently Deleted to get the space back. Export anything you want to keep first.

Steps

  1. Photos → Albums → Videos (also check Slo-mo and Screen Recordings).
  2. Scan for the longest durations. Tap the info (i) button on a video to check details.
  3. To keep a video, share or export the original to iCloud Drive, a computer or another storage first.
  4. Select the videos you do not need and tap Trash.
  5. Empty Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted.

Heavy formats add up: one source notes that 60 seconds of 4K footage can use close to half a gigabyte on the phone. Settings → Camera → Record Video lets you change the recording format for future clips.

Doing this with Swipeo

Swipeo has a Large videos set, so the heavy ones come first. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

Download on theApp Store

FAQ

Does Photos sort videos by size?

Most guides say no. The Files app can sort by size for files, but not Photos items.

Why is my storage still full after deleting videos?

They are still in Recently Deleted for 30 days, or iCloud sync has not finished.

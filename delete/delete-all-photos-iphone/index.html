How to delete all photos from iPhone

Back up first, check iCloud Photos, then delete and empty Recently Deleted.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerSelect your photos in Photos, tap Trash, then empty Recently Deleted. If iCloud Photos is on, the photos are deleted from iCloud and your other devices too, so back up anything you need before you start.

Steps

  1. Back up what you want to keep (computer, external drive or another service).
  2. Check Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos. If it is on, deleting here deletes everywhere.
  3. In Photos, select the photos (use albums with Select All, or drag-select) and tap Trash.
  4. Open Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted, unlock it, tap Select → Delete All.

If you only want to free storage and keep your photos, read Optimize iPhone Storage first: deleting is the one action you cannot take back after 30 days.

Doing this with Swipeo

If you do not really want everything gone, review and keep the good ones, delete the rest. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Will deleting all photos from iPhone delete them from iCloud?

If iCloud Photos is on, yes: the deletion syncs to iCloud and your other devices.

Can I recover them afterwards?

For 30 days via Recently Deleted. After that they are gone unless you have a separate backup.

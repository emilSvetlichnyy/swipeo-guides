How to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

The Duplicates album handles exact copies; similar shots need a manual pass or an app.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOpen Photos → Albums → Utilities → Duplicates, tap Select, choose the items and tap Merge. The built-in tool only finds exact or near-exact copies. Near-identical shots taken a second apart are usually not counted as duplicates.

Steps

  1. Open Photos → Albums and scroll to Utilities.
  2. Tap Duplicates (iOS 16 and later).
  3. Tap Merge next to a set, or Select → Merge for several.
  4. Open Recently Deleted and delete the discarded copies to free space.

What Merge does

Merge keeps one version and moves the other copies to Recently Deleted. It does not free space until they are deleted from that album.

What it misses

Burst-like sequences, edited copies and slightly different angles are not exact duplicates. See similar photos and burst photos.

FAQ

Do I need an app to remove duplicates?

No for exact copies: the Duplicates album is built in. An app helps with near-duplicates.

Why is the Duplicates album empty?

Either there are no exact copies, or the library is still being analysed. It also needs iOS 16 or later.

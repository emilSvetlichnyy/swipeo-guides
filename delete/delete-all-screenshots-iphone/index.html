How to delete all screenshots on iPhone at once

Screenshots have their own album, so bulk deletion takes under a minute.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerIn Photos go to Albums → Media Types → Screenshots, tap Select → Select All, tap the Trash icon and confirm. Then empty Recently Deleted, because the storage is not returned until the screenshots leave that album.

Steps

  1. Open Photos → Albums and scroll to Media Types.
  2. Tap Screenshots, then Select in the top right.
  3. Tap Select All (or pick specific screenshots) and tap the Trash icon.
  4. Confirm Delete, then empty Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted.

Keep a few?

Before Select All, scroll once and tap the heart on the screenshots you want to keep (receipts, tickets, maps). Favorites are easy to filter later. Another route is a Shortcut that deletes old screenshots only: see this guide.

Doing this with Swipeo

Screenshots are usually the fastest clean-up: swipe through them, keep the few that matter, delete the rest. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

FAQ

Where are screenshots stored on iPhone?

In the Photos app under Albums → Media Types → Screenshots.

Why did my storage not change?

Deleted screenshots sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Delete them there to free space.

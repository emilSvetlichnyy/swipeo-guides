How to find and delete similar photos on iPhone

Apple's Duplicates album only catches exact copies, so similar shots take a different approach.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerPhotos finds exact duplicates only. For similar shots, scroll the Library in Days view and compare, mark your best shots with a heart, delete the rest, or use an app that groups similar photos taken at the same time.

Steps

  1. Open Photos → Library → Days to see shots grouped by day.
  2. Tap Select and tap the best photo of each near-identical group with a heart (Favorite).
  3. Delete the non-favorites from each group.
  4. Empty Recently Deleted to free the space.

The Map view can also help: similar photos tend to share one location stamp. Do the pass in short sessions so you do not rush.

FAQ

Is there an Apple tool for similar photos?

Not for near-identical shots. Apple's Duplicates covers exact copies.

Are cleaner apps reliable at this?

Results vary by app. Check the App Store privacy label and review what the app suggests before deleting.

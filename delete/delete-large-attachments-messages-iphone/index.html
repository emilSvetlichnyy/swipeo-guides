How to delete large attachments in Messages on iPhone

Remove the heavy media and keep the conversations.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerGo to Settings → General → iPhone Storage, open Messages (or the recommendation Review Large Attachments), review photos, videos and GIFs, and delete the ones you do not need. The text of your chats stays.

Steps

  1. Settings → General → iPhone Storage.
  2. Tap Messages (or the Review Large Attachments recommendation).
  3. Browse Top Conversations, Photos, Videos, GIFs & Stickers.
  4. Swipe left on an item or tap Edit, then delete.

If Messages is synced with iCloud, deleting an attachment also removes the cloud copy. Free space can take a few minutes to update.

FAQ

Will my conversations be deleted?

No. This only removes the attachments, per the guides.

Why can I not delete an attachment?

An Apple Community thread suggests restarting the iPhone first.

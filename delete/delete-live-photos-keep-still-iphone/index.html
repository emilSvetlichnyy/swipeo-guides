How to delete Live Photos but keep the still photo

Make a still copy first, then delete the Live original. Duplicating alone frees nothing.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerGo to Albums → Media Types → Live Photos, tap Select, choose the photos, tap the ⋯ menu → Duplicate → Duplicate as Still Photo, then delete the original Live Photos and empty Recently Deleted. Check your own storage afterwards: one user reported the still was the same size as the original.

Steps

  1. Photos → Albums → Live Photos.
  2. Tap Select and pick the Live Photos you want to convert.
  3. Tap ⋯ → Duplicate → Duplicate as Still Photo.
  4. Delete the original Live Photos (they stay until you do).
  5. Empty Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted.

Test on a few photos first and compare Settings → General → iPhone Storage before and after, because the saving depends on the photo.

FAQ

Does duplicating as still free space?

Not by itself: the original Live Photo remains until you delete it.

Do I lose the video part?

In the still copy, yes. The motion is not kept.

How to delete burst photos on iPhone

Pick the keepers inside a burst, then remove the rest.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOpen the burst in Photos, tap Select, tap the frames you want to keep, tap Done and choose Keep Only Favorites. The other frames are removed to Recently Deleted. Empty that album to free the space.

Steps

  1. Open the burst (it looks like a stack of photos) and tap Select.
  2. Tap the frames you want. Gray dots below thumbnails are Apple's suggestions.
  3. Tap Done, then choose to keep only your favorite(s).
  4. Empty Recently Deleted to free storage.

To avoid accidental bursts, turn on Use Volume Up for Burst in Settings → Camera or switch burst behaviour off.

Doing this with Swipeo

For the rest of your library, go photo by photo. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone. The free version has a daily limit on deletions. Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Why do burst photos take so much space?

A burst stores many frames from one moment. Keeping one or two usually frees most of it.

A burst will not delete. What now?

One reported workaround is to quit and reopen Photos and try again. It is an anecdote, not an official fix.

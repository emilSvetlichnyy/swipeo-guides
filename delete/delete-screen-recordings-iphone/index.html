How to delete screen recordings on iPhone

They live in their own album, which makes them easy to clear in bulk.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerIn Photos open Albums → Media Types → Screen Recordings, tap Select, pick the recordings (or Select All), tap Trash, then empty Recently Deleted.

Steps

  1. Photos → Albums → Media Types → Screen Recordings.
  2. Tap Select; scan durations and pick the ones to delete.
  3. Tap Trash and confirm.
  4. Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

Screen recordings are long and usually disposable, which is why guides point to this album first when you need space fast.

Doing this with Swipeo

FAQ

Do screen recordings count as videos?

Yes. They are saved in Photos and also appear in Videos.

Can I delete just the old ones?

Sort by date in the album and select the older block with drag-select.

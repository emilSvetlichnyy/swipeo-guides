How long do deleted photos stay on iPhone?

30 days in Recently Deleted, unless you delete them there sooner.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerApple says items in Recently Deleted stay for 30 days, after which they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. You can remove them sooner with Select → Delete All, which also frees the storage.
DayState
0You delete a photo. It moves to Recently Deleted and still uses space
1–29You can Recover it or Delete it for good
30It is removed automatically

The exact per-photo countdown is not clearly documented; an Apple Community thread asks about it without a definitive answer, so do not rely on the last day.

Doing this with Swipeo

Swipeo adds its own short trash before the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so there are two chances to change your mind.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Can I change the 30 days?

Not in the settings that Apple documents.

Does iCloud keep photos longer?

A community answer says iCloud Photos does not keep them in Recently Deleted longer than the phone does. Treat that as unofficial.

Why is Photos taking so much storage on my iPhone?

Usually videos and high-resolution originals, plus some overhead you cannot see.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerMost of the space is usually videos and high-resolution photos. Even with Optimize iPhone Storage on, the phone keeps smaller versions and the Photos total includes database and support files. Messages images may also be counted.

Steps

  1. Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Photos: note the size and the recommendations.
  2. Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos: confirm Optimize iPhone Storage is selected.
  3. Delete the largest videos and duplicates, then empty Recently Deleted.
  4. Check Messages attachments separately.

Several Apple Community threads report a large Photos number with a modest library. Causes reported: previews kept by Optimize, support files, and sometimes a display glitch after an update. Treat the glitch reports as anecdotes.

FAQ

Why is Photos bigger than my photo count suggests?

Video and originals dominate. Database files add overhead.

Does Recently Deleted count?

Yes, until the items are permanently deleted.

iPhone storage full: free up space used by photos

A quick order of operations that works before you delete memories.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerCheck Settings → General → iPhone Storage, turn on Optimize iPhone Storage if you use iCloud Photos, delete the biggest items (long videos, screenshots, duplicates), then empty Recently Deleted. Deleting alone does not free space until that last step.

Steps

  1. Settings → General → iPhone Storage: see what is big and read the recommendations.
  2. Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos: choose Optimize iPhone Storage if you have enough iCloud space.
  3. Delete screenshots, duplicates and long videos.
  4. Empty Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted.
  5. Check Messages attachments and offload unused apps.
ActionFrees space?
Delete photosOnly after Recently Deleted is emptied
Optimize iPhone StorageYes: keeps smaller versions, originals in iCloud
Offload unused appsYes, keeps app data
Review large attachmentsYes

FAQ

Why is my iPhone storage full with few photos?

Videos, Messages attachments, apps and system data count too. Check the iPhone Storage breakdown.

Is iCloud a replacement for local space?

Optimized photos still keep smaller local copies, so it reduces but does not remove local use.

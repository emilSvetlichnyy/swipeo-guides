iPhone storage not going down after deleting photos

Nine times in ten the photos are still in Recently Deleted.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerDeleted photos keep using space until they leave Recently Deleted (up to 30 days). Open Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All, wait for it to finish and restart. If it still does not move, check iCloud Photos sync and delete in smaller batches.

Steps

  1. Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted (unlock it).
  2. Tap Select → Delete All. If the album is very full, delete in small batches.
  3. Restart the iPhone and recheck Settings → General → iPhone Storage (it can lag a few minutes).
  4. Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos: check that sync has no errors, then delete again and choose Delete From All Devices.

FAQ

Why did deleted photos come back?

iCloud sync can restore items in some cases. A few threads report photos reappearing in Recently Deleted without a confirmed fix.

How long until the space returns?

Immediately after the items are deleted from Recently Deleted, though the display can lag.

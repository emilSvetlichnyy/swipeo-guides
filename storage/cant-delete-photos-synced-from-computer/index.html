Can't delete photos on iPhone: trash icon greyed out

The photos came from a computer sync, so the phone is not allowed to delete them.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerA greyed-out Trash usually means the photos were synced from a computer. Delete them by changing the sync on that computer (Finder or iTunes: untick the photos or albums, then sync), or, if they came from a different computer, turn off iCloud Photos on the iPhone.

Steps

  1. Connect the iPhone to the computer you synced from.
  2. In Finder (Mac) or iTunes/Apple Devices (Windows), open the Photos tab.
  3. Untick the albums or folders you no longer want, then sync.
  4. If the photos came from another computer, try Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos → turn off, after making sure you have a copy.

Back up first: if the phone holds the only copy, removing it deletes your only copy. Do not turn off iCloud Photos casually: if you delete before turning it off, items can be removed everywhere.

FAQ

Why is the trash icon missing?

Synced items are managed by the computer, not by the iPhone.

Is there a way without a computer?

Reports mention turning off iCloud Photos, but it carries risk. Use a backup first.

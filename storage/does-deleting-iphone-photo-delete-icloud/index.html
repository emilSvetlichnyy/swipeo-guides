Does deleting a photo on iPhone delete it from iCloud?

With iCloud Photos on, yes: the delete is synced everywhere.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerYes, if iCloud Photos is on. A photo deleted on iPhone is removed from iCloud and your other devices signed in to the same account. It sits in Recently Deleted for 30 days first. If iCloud Photos is off, the delete only affects the phone.
iCloud PhotosDelete on iPhone
OnRemoved from iCloud and other devices (30 days in Recently Deleted)
OffRemoved only from this iPhone

Apple's prompt may offer Delete From All Devices in some flows. Read it before confirming.

FAQ

Can I delete only from the iPhone?

Not while iCloud Photos is syncing. Optimize iPhone Storage keeps the original in iCloud while freeing space on the phone.

How do I check if iCloud Photos is on?

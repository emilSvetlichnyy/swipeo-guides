How to empty Recently Deleted on iPhone

This is the step that actually returns the storage.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerPhotos → Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted → unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or passcode → Select → Delete All. After that the photos are permanently gone and the space is freed.

Steps

  1. Open Photos → Albums → scroll to Utilities.
  2. Tap Recently Deleted and unlock it.
  3. Tap Select, then Delete All (or choose specific items and tap Delete).
  4. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

Anything left there is removed automatically after 30 days. If you are not sure about some photos, tap Recover on those first.

Doing this with Swipeo

After you finish a cleaning session in Swipeo, empty Recently Deleted to get the space back.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Can I get photos back after Delete All?

Not from the phone. Only a separate backup may help.

Is it safe to delete everything in Recently Deleted?

Yes if you have checked it. Scan the thumbnails once first.

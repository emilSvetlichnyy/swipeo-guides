How to recover deleted photos on iPhone

Within 30 days it is two taps. After that you need a backup.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOpen Photos → Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted, unlock it, tap Select, choose the photos and tap Recover. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and only a separate backup can bring them back.

Steps

  1. Photos → Albums → Utilities → Recently Deleted.
  2. Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or passcode.
  3. Tap Select, pick the photos and tap Recover at the bottom.
  4. The photos return to their original place in your library.

If you used a cleaner app with its own trash, check that first. It may still hold the items before they reach Recently Deleted. Beyond 30 days, community answers suggest a device or iCloud backup, which is not official guidance.

FAQ

Can I recover photos deleted from Recently Deleted?

Not from the phone. Only an earlier backup may contain them.

Will recovered photos return to iCloud?

With iCloud Photos on, recovered items sync again like normal library items.

How to free up iPhone storage without deleting photos

Four settings that can give back gigabytes without touching a single memory.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerTurn on Optimize iPhone Storage for iCloud Photos, offload unused apps (data is kept), review large Messages attachments and clear Safari website data. None of these delete your photos.

Steps

  1. Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos → Optimize iPhone Storage.
  2. Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Offload apps you rarely use.
  3. iPhone Storage → Messages → Review Large Attachments.
  4. Settings → Apps → Safari → Clear History and Website Data (menu path varies by iOS version).

Optimize depends on iCloud space, and it is not a replacement for a backup. If these steps are not enough, the next options are deleting large videos and screenshots.

Doing this with Swipeo

When settings are not enough, cleaning the clutter is the next step. Open the app, swipe or tap to keep or delete, and check Swipeo's trash before anything reaches the iPhone's Recently Deleted.

FAQ

What frees the most space without deleting photos?

Optimize iPhone Storage can free the most when you have a large library and enough iCloud space.

Does offloading an app delete its data?

No. Offloading removes the app but keeps documents and data.

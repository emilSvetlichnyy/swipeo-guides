Optimize iPhone Storage vs Download and Keep Originals

One saves phone space, the other saves convenience and offline access.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerOptimize iPhone Storage keeps smaller versions on the phone and full-resolution files in iCloud, which frees space. Download and Keep Originals stores full-resolution files on the phone, so photos open instantly and offline but use more space.
Optimize iPhone StorageDownload and Keep Originals
Phone spaceLessMore
Offline originalsNo: downloaded on demandYes
Needs iCloud spaceYes, for all originalsYes, iCloud still stores them
Best forBig libraries, small phonesEditing offline, large storage

Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos. On newer iOS versions the Photos settings may also appear under Settings → Apps → Photos.

Doing this with Swipeo

Optimize helps with space; cleaning helps with clutter. Swipeo is for the second: removing what you do not want in the first place.

Download on theApp Store

Swipeo runs on iPhone and iPad, free to download with in-app purchases (what the app does). Items you delete go to Swipeo's own trash first; emptying it moves them to the iPhone's Recently Deleted, so iOS rules such as the 30-day window and iCloud sync still apply.

FAQ

Does Optimize delete my originals?

No. Originals remain in iCloud, as long as you have iCloud space.

Does Optimize free everything?

Not fully: the device keeps smaller versions.

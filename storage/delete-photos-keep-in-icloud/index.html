How to delete photos from iPhone but keep them in iCloud

You cannot, if iCloud Photos is on. Optimize Storage is the supported way.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Swipeo

Short answerWith iCloud Photos on, deleting a photo on iPhone deletes it from iCloud and your other devices. To free iPhone space and keep originals, choose Optimize iPhone Storage: the phone keeps smaller versions and iCloud keeps the full-resolution files.

Steps

  1. Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Photos.
  2. Make sure Sync this iPhone is on.
  3. Select Optimize iPhone Storage.
  4. Give it time to upload and replace originals with smaller copies.

Forum answers add that Apple offers no cloud-only storage, and turning iCloud Photos off to delete can bring photos back when you turn it on again. If you want a separate copy, export to a computer first.

Doing this with Swipeo

FAQ

Is Optimize iPhone Storage the same as deleting?

No. Nothing is lost: full-resolution files stay in iCloud.

What if my iCloud is full?

Optimize depends on iCloud space. You may need a larger plan or a separate backup.
